# Technical details for “Power Estimation for Between-Cluster Randomized Factorial Trials with Binary Outcomes”

This website accompanies the poster **“Power Estimation for Between-Cluster Randomized Factorial Trials with Binary Outcomes”** presented at the *2026 Conference on Intervention Optimization*.  

Its purpose is to provide brief technical background and implementation details that could not fit on the poster, including:

- Clarification of marginal vs latent ICC
- The general idea behind our power approximation
- The exact GLMM-based Monte Carlo implementation
- Simulation settings and additional results
